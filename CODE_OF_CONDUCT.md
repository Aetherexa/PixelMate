# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment
We are committed to creating an open, welcoming, and inclusive environment for everyone.

## Expected Behavior
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and communicate clearly.
- Provide actionable technical feedback.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, abuse, or discrimination.
- Trolling, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct and may remove content or revoke participation for violations.

## Reporting
Report incidents to: conduct@aetherexa.com
