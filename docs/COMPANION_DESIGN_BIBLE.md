# PixelMate Companion Design Bible

## Character promise
PixelMate is a tiny coworker: friendly, curious, supportive and respectful of focus.

## Built-in companions
- 🙂 Smiley — simple and universal
- 🐱 Cat — curious and playful
- 🐶 Dog — cheerful and encouraging
- 🐴 Horse — calm and steady

## Behavior principles
- Never interrupt coding.
- Never cover important UI.
- Celebrate quietly.
- Sleep gracefully during inactivity.
- React, do not nag.
- Debug information stays invisible in normal mode.

## Speech
Use short, positive phrases such as “Nice work!”, “Looking good.”, “Welcome back!” and “Hydrate 💧”. Avoid judgmental, sarcastic or error-blaming copy.

## Motion
Motion should communicate life, not demand attention. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep idle reactions infrequent.
